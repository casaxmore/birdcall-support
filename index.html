Frequently asked questions

How do I identify a bird?

In Home, open Bird Identifier. Take a photo, choose one from your library, record a call, or import audio. Allow camera or microphone access in Settings if needed. Suggestions are generated on your device and may be incorrect. Compare them with the catalogue images and calls.

How do I play the Daily Bird Challenge?

Open Home, then Daily Bird Challenge. Listen to the call and choose one of the three birds. After answering you can replay the call, open the species profile, and see your total score. The challenge is free, and your results are saved on your device.

Why can’t I hear a call?

Turn up your device’s media volume and check whether audio is going to headphones or a Bluetooth speaker. Try the play button on an unlocked bird profile.

What is included in Premium?

Premium is a one-time purchase for the full catalogue, unlimited photo and sound identification, and an ad-free experience. The free version includes five birds and a daily challenge. Optional rewarded ads can unlock a selected bird for one hour or identification for 30 minutes.

How do I restore Premium?

Open Settings in the app and choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase.
